01 · The big idea

Three frameworks. Three clear jobs.

Explain the purpose of each framework in everyday language. They work together, but they are not the same thing.

WHY

NEP 2020

Sets the national vision: children should understand, apply, communicate, collaborate and solve real-life problems.

HOW

NCF

Guides curriculum and teaching: use age-appropriate activities, objects, pictures, conversation, practice and reflection.

EVIDENCE

HPC

Helps teachers notice a child’s progress, strengths and next learning needs, not only a final score.

NEP sets the direction → NCF guides the learning → HPC records progress
02 · The learning shift

From memorising to understanding

These are illustrative teaching patterns, not a claim that every classroom before or after 2020 teaches in the same way.

A rule-first lesson

“Copy the sum. Find the answer.”

  • Teacher writes 24 + 13.
  • Children copy the steps.
  • Practice focuses mainly on the correct answer.
A learning-first lesson

“Build it. Explain it. Use it.”

  • Children make 24 and 13 with counters.
  • They bundle ten ones into a ten.
  • They draw, write 24 + 13 = 37 and explain their thinking.

NEP 2020 is a policy vision. Implementation differs across schools, states, resources and teacher preparation.

04 · How to teach it

Use the same simple learning path

Move from something the child can touch, to something they can see, to the maths symbol.

1 · DO

Use real objects

Bottle caps, pencils, blocks, seeds or paper circles. Let children move and count them.

2 · SHOW

Draw the idea

Make dots, groups, rows or a number line. Ask the child to explain the picture.

3 · WRITE

Use the symbol

Connect the activity to +, −, × or ÷. Ask the learner to explain what the symbol means.

Adjust for the learner’s stage

Use small numbers, objects, oral questions and playful counting. Keep the activity short and concrete.

Notice more than the answer

ReflectionNeeds prompts.Names one thing learned.Explains what worked and what to try next.

HPC can help us notice

Understanding, reasoning, communication, confidence, participation and progress over time.

HPC is not

A label that defines a child, a one-time judgement or a way to compare children’s worth.

06 · Zoom presentation

A clear 12-minute plan

Let the audience experience the activity first. Then explain the framework connection.

2:30–7:30Show the activity

7:30–9:30Explain the links

NEP vision, NCF teaching and HPC evidence.

9:30–12:00Reflect

Share one observation and one next step.

READY-TO-SAY OPENING

“Today I will show how children can understand the four basic mathematical operations through familiar, practical examples. NEP 2020 gives us the wider vision of meaningful learning. NCF helps us plan how children experience the concept, and HPC helps us notice their progress.”

READY-TO-SAY CLOSING

“The answer matters, but the child’s thinking matters too. When children use objects, explain their strategy and connect maths to daily life, we can see what they understand and what they need next.”

07 · Practical homework

5 MIN

Record what you notice
